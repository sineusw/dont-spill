Privacy Policy

Don't Spill · Petty Games Studio · Last updated November 24, 2026

What we collect

Don't Spill does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your progress (coins, upgrades, settings, scores) is saved only on your device.

Leaderboards

If you choose to post a score, we store the nickname you type and your score so other players can see the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words.

Challenge links

Challenge links you share contain only a game seed, your score and your nickname.

Purchases and ads

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. If ads are shown, they may be provided by an ad partner that follows Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules; you can decline tracking when asked.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or delete the app, to remove all data stored on your device.

Contact

Questions? Contact Petty Games Studio through the support link on our App Store page.
