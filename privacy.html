Privacy Policy

Don't Spill · Petty Games Studio · Last updated November 24, 2026

What we collect

Don't Spill does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your progress (coins, upgrades, settings, scores) is saved only on your device.

Leaderboards

If you choose to post a score, we store the nickname you type and your score so other players can see the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words.

Challenge links

Challenge links you share contain only a game seed, your score and your nickname.

Ads

The app can show optional video ads (for example, to double your pay after a shift). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and crash/performance data to deliver and measure ads. We don't ask to track you, so ads are not personalized using data from other companies' apps and websites. See how Google uses data.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center, your shift scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules Dinner Rush and daily reward reminders on your phone. No data leaves your device for this.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or delete the app, to remove all data stored on your device.

Contact

Questions? Contact Petty Games Studio through the support link on our App Store page.
